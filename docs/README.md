# Project Buddy

These docs describe Project Buddy: its scope and operating practices. GitHub issues track remaining work; the repo's `README.md` covers setup and commands.

## Product

- [Button system](button-system.md): Letterpress action roles, color vocabulary and control inventory.

- [Appearance](appearance.md): the three textured swatches and browser preferences.

- [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md): the domain words that code, copy, prompts and docs share.
- [Local constructs](local-constructs.md): MUD-inspired commands, the no-model-request policy and the retained harness.
- [Page workspaces](page-workspaces.md): quest overviews, linked sections and the Timer panel.
- [Premade quests](premade-quests.md): the four starting points, Dailies, and the one quest start.
- [Quest status](quest-status.md): completing, abandoning and restoring quests, with construct approval.
- [Conversations](chats.md): the Quests landing page, quest conversations and reply behavior.
- [Prompts](prompts.md): editable construct and spell fields, and their storage.
- [Spells](spellbook.md): Tasks and Brainstorm modes, casts, stopping and message capture.
- [Dailies](dailies.md): Start Day and its Undo, recurring tasks, streaks and the day grid.
- [Quest tasks](quest-tasks.md): a quest's tasks of remaining work, the construct's `change_tasks` tool, and the Tasks section.
- [Quest ideas](quest-ideas.md): saved possibilities and moving an idea into Tasks.
- [Quest overview](quest-overview.md): Next task, quest sections, spell conversations, and the Timer panel.
- [Progress timeline](progress-timeline.md): recording, editing, and deleting milestones.
- [Profile visiting days](profile.md): distinct days signed in, the UTC boundary and tracking start.
- [Experience](experience.md): ability points, experience and circles, and what gains and takes back points.
- [Quick Notes](quick-notes.md): saving a short note to a quest from the site panel or directly from the quest's Quick Notes bucket.
- [Spellbook’s Forgotten tab](retirement.md): retiring, restoring, and permanently deleting own spells.
- [Saved images](images.md): conversation images, owner access and deletion.
- [Construct visuals](construct-visuals.md): shared artwork, metadata, rendering primitives and asset ownership.
- [Place icons](place-icons.md): the shared Lucide mapping, sizing and attribution.
- [Installed app](notifications.md): installing on a phone's Home Screen.
- [Timer](timer.md): the repeating work and break timer, its links and the count of others following a schedule.
- [Multi-user quests](multi-user-quests.md): shared quest permissions, accounts created by invitations and acceptance through the Pending tab or invitation links.
