# Constructs

A quest's construct is the character shown with its performances' replies. A new quest gets built-in Vellum, and keeps it. Constructs are built in, and their pages are read-only.

## use_case_view_character_profile

The user meets the quest's construct.

- **Prerequisites:** an Active quest open to the user.
- **Steps:** open the portrait and name control after the breadcrumbs, before the quest-panel control.
- **Outcome:** a named profile shows the quest's construct's artwork, temperament, approach to quests and a representative sample line. Opening it makes no model call. Keyboard focus stays inside the profile; Escape and Close return focus to its trigger. The profile fits a 320px phone, scrolls when needed and leaves the composer usable after closing.
