# Theme redesign aesthetics review

Project Buddy serves people who use quests, conversations with constructs, and a Timer to organize real work. I read the brief as asking for a calm, trustworthy workspace with enough fantasy to make the constructs and appearances feel particular to Project Buddy. I assumed people will spend more time reading conversations and editing lists than looking at the Timer. I weighed sustained reading and visual hierarchy accordingly.

I reviewed revision `db79f47e` in headless Chromium 156.0.8078.4 on macOS. Playwright opened the static files directly at 1280 × 900 and 360 × 780 in Ivory Vellum, Soot & Brass and Espresso Leather. The comparison sheets show the first viewport-height portion of each mockup and its current capture. The batch screenshot tool extends the viewport to include long content, which moves fixed and sticky elements in those shots. I repeated selected views at their original heights to judge those elements. I measured the swatches at a supplementary 320 px width. The current screenshots came from the committed capture, whose inventory identifies revision `916daaa2`. See [capture inventory](../../capture/inventory.md) and [review brief](../../BRIEF.md).

## Findings

### The live reply sits below the fold while Stop reply is already visible

- **Screen and state.** Quest conversation, loading reply. At 360 px in all three appearances, the thinking indicator starts around 2,547 px below the top of the document while the sticky composer shows Stop reply at the bottom of the first viewport. At 1280 px the current capture also opens at the latest exchange, while the proposal opens on old messages.
- **Use case.** `use_case_construct_presence`, `use_case_follow_latest_messages` and `use_case_stop_reply` in [chats.md](../../../../apps/web/use-cases/chats.md).
- **Evidence.** Open `quest--loading-reply.html?bare&appearance=dark&width=360` at 360 × 780, then scroll to the end. The small dots and explicit “Vellum is thinking...” line are clear once visible. Computed animation changes from `working-dot` to `none` with reduced motion in all three appearances. The default view shows the opening message and commands, and Stop reply has no corresponding visible status. [Espresso Leather on load](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/viewport-quest--loading-reply--dark--360.png) · [After scrolling](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/bottom-quest--loading-reply--dark--360.png) · [Current and mock at 360](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/compare-360-2.jpg) · [Current and mock at 1280](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/compare-1280-2.jpg)
- **Judgment.** Distracting craft defect in state composition. The composer shows urgency without the construct feedback that explains it.
- **Recommendation.** Frame the mockup at the active reply on load. In the port, preserve the existing conversation viewport and latest-message behavior, including the ability to scroll back while a reply is underway. The shared composer rule supplies sticky placement. The quest view supplies scroll state. [Quest view partial](../../views-src/_partials/quest-page.html) · [Component CSS](../../system/components.css#L2133)

### The destructive action opens the vertical stack on phones

- **Screen and state.** Delete milestone confirmation at 360 px, all three appearances. Current capture places Keep it above Delete milestone. The mockup places the red Delete milestone above Keep it, even though the source order is Keep it then Delete milestone.
- **Use case.** `use_case_edit_milestone` in [quests.md](../../../../apps/web/use-cases/quests.md).
- **Evidence.** At 360 × 780 the mockup correctly centers its dialog. The two actions wrap and `flex-wrap: wrap-reverse` places the red action directly below the explanation, with a smaller Keep it button one line lower and pushed to the right. The corner treatment and opaque panel make the alert dialog visually distinct. [Ivory Vellum confirmation](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/viewport-quest--confirm-dialog--light--360.png) · [Current capture comparison](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/compare-360-1.jpg) · [Shared dialog rule](../../system/components.css#L1770)
- **Judgment.** Noticeable craft defect. The inconsistent reading and source order weaken the careful hierarchy expected in a destructive confirmation.
- **Recommendation.** Keep source and visual order aligned in the shared dialog action group. Use an explicit narrow stack with aligned action edges.

### Settings breaks the three-swatch row on phones

- **Screen and state.** Settings at 360 px, all three appearances, resting, selected, focus and tooltip. The same wrap occurs in the 320 px geometry check. The site panel's compact version fits on one row at both phone widths.
- **Use case.** `use_case_choose_theme` and `use_case_use_on_phone` in [appearance.md](../../../../apps/web/use-cases/appearance.md).
- **Evidence.** The 88 px face plus label padding gives three 96 px choices. With two 12 px gaps, the row needs 312 px. The Settings frame provides about 270–272 px at 360. Espresso Leather drops alone onto the next line and its tooltip crosses the lower keyline. The current Settings capture keeps the three previews together. I confirmed the dashed keyboard outline and hover tooltip on native radios in all three appearances. Escape left the tooltip visible while the pointer stayed over the swatch. The CSS supports a dismissed state that the static view did not set. [Espresso Leather tooltip and row](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/viewport-settings--swatch-tooltip--dark--360.png) · [Settings comparison](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/compare-360-4.jpg) · [Swatch CSS](../../system/components.css#L1141)
- **Judgment.** Noticeable craft defect. The three finishes are meant to read as a set. The lone swatch distorts the selector's rhythm and pushes Security down.
- **Recommendation.** Use the compact face size in Settings when the component has insufficient inline room. Preserve the separate solid selection and dashed focus rings and the 44 px target. Keep production Escape behavior when adopting the new markup.

### Phone collection tabs move the primary categories below secondary ones

- **Screen and state.** Quests list and empty list, and Spellbook list and Yours empty, at 360 px in all three appearances.
- **Use cases.** `use_case_view_quests` in [quests.md](../../../../apps/web/use-cases/quests.md) and `use_case_spellbook_page` in [spellbook.md](../../../../apps/web/use-cases/spellbook.md).
- **Evidence.** Abandoned and Pending appear above Active and Completed. In the Spellbook, Forgotten takes an entire row above All, Built-in and Yours. The current captures put the primary categories first in both collections. The shared `wrap-reverse` rule places the first source-order tabs closest to the panel, and the phone flex rule stretches Forgotten across the row. [Quests comparison](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/compare-360-3.jpg) · [Spellbook comparison](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/compare-360-6.jpg) · [Collection CSS](../../system/components.css#L1312)
- **Judgment.** Noticeable craft defect. The large secondary row receives more visual emphasis than the active categories, and the visual reading order differs from the source.
- **Recommendation.** Use a regular wrap or a two-row grid at narrow widths. Use the selected tab's border and fill to connect it visually to the collection even when it sits on the first row.

### The spell editor stretches a short name field across almost the whole desktop

- **Screen and state.** Spell editor and invalid-field state at 1280 px, all three appearances.
- **Use case.** `use_case_customize_spell` in [spellbook.md](../../../../apps/web/use-cases/spellbook.md).
- **Evidence.** In Ivory Vellum the name field spans about 1,096 px. Its label, value, hint and destination radios cluster at the left of the frame. The current editor keeps the form and reply settings on a narrower shared axis. The mockup's separate reply panel and persistent Save spell action improve the distinction between editing work and saving it. In the invalid state the red line and message survive, while the current captured focus ring is absent from the mockup's initial state. [Ivory Vellum editor](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/viewport-spell--editor--light--1280.png) · [Editor comparison](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/compare-1280-5.jpg) · [Invalid-state comparison](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/compare-1280-6.jpg)
- **Judgment.** Noticeable design judgment. Long reply rows benefit from room, while the short metadata looks sparse when it inherits the same measure.
- **Recommendation.** Give the metadata form a readable field measure inside the spell panel and keep the wider layout available to reply rows. Use a shared field-width role if other forms need it. Preserve the invalid field's focus placement in the React port.

### The proposed Profile ring carries progress but loses the second visual scale

- **Screen and state.** Populated Profile at both widths, all three appearances.
- **Use case.** `use_case_experience` and `use_case_experience_ring` in [experience.md](../../../../apps/web/use-cases/experience.md).
- **Evidence.** The current card pairs the circle indicator with a labeled horizontal progress bar. The proposal uses one ring and a single line for experience and the distance to the next circle. The circle number is easier to spot, while the smaller text and thin ring carry the progress information together. The proposal also flattens the current ability-card graphics into text and figures. [Desktop Profile comparison](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/compare-1280-0.jpg) · [Phone Profile comparison](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/compare-360-0.jpg) · [Profile markup](../../views-src/profile--experience.html)
- **Judgment.** Noticeable design judgment. The simpler card fits the book treatment, but progress towards the next circle reads less immediately, especially on a phone.
- **Recommendation.** Decide whether the ring should carry both jobs. If it does, show experience and distance as visibly distinct labels and bind the arc to the actual circle interval in the Profile and header. The prototype hard-codes its arc.

## Component and implementation notes

- The shared frame and rule slots carry the appearances well. Ivory Vellum's fine corners and rubric read as a scribe's page. Soot & Brass has the clearest distinction between burgundy ground and charcoal working surfaces, and its arch motif echoes the Timer. Espresso Leather's gilt rules and seal work on the leather ground, though the header rule, frame corners and speaker labels all call attention at once in long conversations. The Timer comparison confirms that each illustration remains the focal point on its texture. [Timer comparison](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/compare-1280-6.jpg) · [Component specimen, type and actions](/tmp/buddy-aesthetics.xogCGz/specimen-sheet-1280-0.jpg)
- The sampled shared rules use semantic colour and ornament slots without component selectors inside appearance stylesheets. The component catalogue provides the old-role mapping. Some dimensions are still literal despite the brief's semantic requirement. For example, the two swatch sizes and focus offset live in the component, as do the dialog's gap and padding. Keep these explicit component roles with shared defaults in the contract before porting. The generic focus token is solid and the swatch rule independently makes its focus dashed. Confirm which controls need a dashed focus outline under the brief and share the appropriate token. [Component catalogue](../../system/components.md) · [Theme interface](../../system/THEME-INTERFACE.md) · [System tokens](../../system/tokens.css#L47)
- A markup-only migration would preserve neither modal mechanics nor the active conversation framing on its own. The static confirmation uses `role="alertdialog"` with a name and description, and the single-panel layout proves its visual center at actual viewport height. Focus trapping, Escape and focus restoration depend on keeping the app's overlay primitives. The conversation port similarly needs to keep its viewport and mount behavior. The loading dots already respect reduced motion in the prototype. [Confirmation markup](../../views-src/_partials/quest-page.html) · [Implementation plan](../../IMPLEMENTATION.md)
- The new Lumen avatar cut retains the face and chapter ring at 32 and 40 px across backgrounds. The larger render shows the full orbital lines, and the shared paint roles give the same drawing a legible appearance on each material. The Vellum profile replaces the four current samples with Portrait and Avatar. ## Use cases outside the critical views

- **Accounts and access.** Sign-up, expired or reused links, sign-in throttling, session expiry and signed-out return are absent as separate states. Fields, notices and actions can cover their visual surfaces. Private data enforcement has no visual surface of its own.
- **Quests and membership.** Premade quest browsing, quest creation dialog, Dailies, Start Day and Undo, recurring tasks and streaks, Pending invitations, members, roles, removal and alternate quest statuses lack complete fixtures. Collection tabs, rows, notices and dialogs cover most of these. The day grid and membership-specific layouts need their own review.
- **Conversations and spells.** Spell casts and their captured messages, storage failures, saved-image loading, crowded replies, stop and resume sequences, and the Forgotten lifecycle need fixtures. Existing Markdown, notices, menus, loading roles and lists give these a starting point. Image treatment and longer-lived error feedback need rendered examples.
- **Timer and platform.** The break phase, actual preferences drawer, copied and failed feedback, invalid link and signed-out running state lack proposed comparisons. Drawer, action and notice roles can cover the related surfaces. Installed-app instructions and Credits need page-level fixtures. CLI and API operations and deployment upkeep have no site visual surface of their own.

## Decisions and follow-through

- _Apply_ the initial scroll position for active conversation states in [the quest view partial](../../views-src/_partials/quest-page.html). Keep the existing scroll and sticky composer behavior during the React migration.
- _Apply_ source-order wrapping to narrow dialogs and collection tabs, and use compact swatches in narrow Settings contexts. Align decorative and focus dimensions with semantic roles in [components.css](../../system/components.css) and [tokens.css](../../system/tokens.css).
- _Apply_ a readable metadata field width in [the spell editor source](../../views-src/spell--editor.html). Preserve the error field's focus on the production path.
- _Decide_ whether the Profile ring alone communicates progress and whether sign-in success should replace the submitted address and retry action. Track both choices in [IMPLEMENTATION.md](../../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
- _Decide_ how much book ornament to carry into frequently used screens. Keep the high-detail artwork and emblems for the Timer, sign-in, empty states and swatches. Consider one focal illustration per empty state and a quieter Espresso Leather trail rule for long reading sessions. [Theme direction](../../themes/espresso-leather/DIRECTION.md) · [Ivory Vellum direction](../../themes/ivory-vellum/DIRECTION.md)
- _Skip_ changing the background materials. The current illustrations already give each appearance a distinct visual anchor.

The consistent frame construction, material-specific swatches and transparent Timer illustrations are the strongest parts of the proposal. The open tone question is how often the full ornament should appear in a workspace people use throughout the day. Browser and device coverage is limited to the stated Chromium viewports and the supplementary swatch-width check. Tablet, real touch, live interactions, dynamic data and exhaustive component states remain outside the evidence. Playwright was closed after capture. Screenshots and scripts remain in the linked temporary directory. No application process, account, database record, commit or push was created.
